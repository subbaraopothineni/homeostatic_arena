# Suggested reviewers (optional; your decision)

Many journals let you suggest 3–5 reviewers and name people to exclude. Editors are free
to ignore suggestions.

**Rules of thumb:**
- Suggest people who are expert in the topic.
- Do not suggest people with a conflict of interest: co-authors or close collaborators in the last 3–5 years, colleagues at your institution, or supervisors and students.
- Take each person's current institutional e-mail from their own institution's web page, and check it yourself.

No contact details are given below on purpose, to avoid inventing or misattributing them.
